READING THE COMBINATIONS

One advert,
several capabilities

The percentage shows how often a second skill appears among adverts with the skill in focus.

Relative frequency divides that percentage by the second skill’s share across all selected adverts. A value of 2× means it appears twice as often.

Both measures describe co-occurrence. They do not establish a wage premium or a causal relationship.

Only pairs seen together in at least 10 adverts appear here.

ABOUT THE DATA

What these numbers can tell us

Adverts, skills and denominators

The unit is a unique advert ID on PNGworkforce.com. An advert may recruit for several positions. Reposts with different IDs remain separate. These counts do not measure the number of workers employers intend to hire.

The skills run processed 3,964 records and classified nine as non-job notices. All charts start with the remaining 3,955 adverts, including 59 with no assigned skill. Filters then define the denominator.

A category counts at most once per advert. A family counts any of its included categories once per advert. Percentages can sum to more than 100% because an advert can contain several skills.

Two levels of ESCO

The dashboard covers the 74 second-level S1–S8 categories in the supplied codebook, grouped into eight families. It does not cover every concept in ESCO’s wider knowledge, language and transversal branches.

A code ending in .0 is a general category within its family, not the family total. Family totals combine all included child categories and remove overlaps.

Evidence and research selections

The model, gpt-6-luna, selected skills required or strongly implied by the text. “Explicit only” retains assignments marked explicit by the model. This is a sensitivity check, not an independent verification of accuracy.

Research shortlist contains the 47 coloured categories in the first S1–S8 list in the supplied Word document, combining all colours. Original study contains the 24 categories from the emailed Indeed prompt. Neither changes which adverts enter the denominator.

Each selection has a source quote. Structural and quote checks passed, but the pilot review found some over-inference, including assigning hands-on activities to supervisors. The browser lets you review these decisions directly.

Coverage matters

Geography uses named work provinces and regions from the classification pipeline. PNG-wide adverts are not assigned to every province. Multi-province adverts count once in each named province. Missing locations are kept in the sample.

Online recruitment represents the employers and roles using this website. It is not a representative sample of PNG employment, and differences in advert detail can affect the skills identified.
