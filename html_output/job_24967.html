ABOUT BALIMO COLLEGE:

Balimo College is Western Province’s newest Higher Education Institute. In 2025, Balimo College opened the new School of Nursing and commenced a 4-year Bachelor of General Nursing program. The College’s vision is to advance Western Province by providing high-quality education, research and community engagement. The institute will foster pathways for teachers and nurses who are committed to improving access to vital services in Western Province.

Balimo College is a safe and supportive place for graduates and other professionals across the Western Province catchment region to return for life-long learning and ongoing professional development. The College will develop a community of education and health professionals committed to innovation, excellence and community advancement; by being the training College of choice for Papua New Guineans.

Balimo College is a PNG Sustainable Development Program (SDP) initiative delivered in partnership with KTF, Divine Word University, and Western Sydney University. Balimo College School of Nursing is an affiliate of Divine Word University.

ABOUT THE ROLE:

The Balimo College School of Nursing Principal is responsible for upholding the College’s Vision, Mission, Core Values, Educational Philosophy and Goals. The Principal will operate within the Balimo College School of Nursing leadership structure as determined by the Chair, Governing Council, to disseminate strong, effective educational leadership, and manage all information technology within the parameters identified in this position description.

The Principal will have an in-depth knowledge of the ethos and purpose of the Balimo College School of Nursing and the principles by which it operates. The Principal will have a firm understanding of the Western Province health context and the objectives of Balimo College School of Nursing to create pathways for rural communities into the field of nursing.
