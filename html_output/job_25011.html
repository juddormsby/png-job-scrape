Join PNG Motors as an Experienced Service Advisor – Be the Voice Our Customers Trust!

Do you thrive on solving problems, love working with people, and have a passion for cars? PNG Motors is looking for a skilled Service Advisor to be the friendly face and trusted voice of our service team. This isn’t just a desk job — it’s about being the bridge between our valued customers and the workshop, ensuring every service experience is smooth, stress-free, and exceeds expectations.

In this front-line role, you’ll be the first person customers turn to when their vehicle needs care. From welcoming them in, to translating technical details into simple language, to making sure their car is ready on time — you’ll be the one they trust to keep them moving.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Greeting customers and guiding them through the service process.

  • Capturing their service needs and preparing accurate job cards.

  • Explaining technical work and estimates in clear, customer-friendly terms.

  • Coordinating with workshop staff to keep everything on track.

  • Handling follow-ups, queries, and concerns with professionalism and care.

  • Keeping all records, systems, and ERA service logs up to date.

  • Promoting additional services that add value and keep vehicles at their best.

What We’re Looking For

  • Previous experience as a Service Advisor (or a similar customer-facing automotive role).

  • Familiarity with dealership processes and ERA systems.

  • A natural communicator who can explain the technical with confidence and clarity.

  • Strong multitasking and time management skills in a busy environment.

  • A professional, empathetic, and customer-first attitude.

Why PNG Motors?

At PNG Motors, you’re not just joining a company — you’re becoming part of a story that’s been driving Papua New Guinea forward for over 50 years. We proudly represent some of the world’s most trusted automotive brands — Hyundai, Mazda, Ford, Suzuki, JMC, and more — and we back them up with an unmatched reputation for service and care.

When you join us, you’ll be part of a supportive, fast-paced team where no two days are the same. You’ll grow your skills, build customer relationships, and help keep PNG on the road.
