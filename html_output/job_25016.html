PNG Motors Automotive Sales Consultants

Share this job:


National Capital District, Papua New Guinea

Automotive

#25016

29th October 2025

www.pngworkforce.com


This Isn’t a Desk Job. It’s a Fast Lane to Success.

Join PNG Motors and sell across PNG’s strongest automotive brands.

The Opportunity

PNG Motors is building its next high-performing sales team, and we’re looking for experienced automotive sales professionals who know how to earn trust, create opportunity, and close with confidence. With Mazda, Hyundai, Suzuki, JMC and Joylong, Suzuki Outboards, and Hyundai Forklifts in one dealership, you’ll have the brand range and credibility to convert more customers, more often.

Who Succeeds Here

This role is for go getters, people with drive and determination who want to be the best. You understand relationships, you respect the follow-up process, and you know that consistency is what separates average salespeople from true performers. If you can build a pipeline and keep deals moving forward, you’ll excel here.

What You Bring

You have recent automotive sales experience, confidence talking with customers in both English and Tok Pisin, and a current driver’s licence. You show up with the right attitude — proactive, disciplined, and focused on results. You don’t need to be pushed — you know how to push yourself.

Why PNG Motors

Here, you get brand recognition, dealership support, aftersales infrastructure, and a national network behind you — giving you a bigger platform to grow your sales career. The better you perform, the more you earn, and the more doors open for leadership and advancement.

Are You Ready to Step Up

This isn’t just a job change — it’s a bigger playing field with a bigger upside.

If you’re sitting at your desk reading this, chances are you’re ready for a change — we want to hear from you!

How to Apply
Send your application letter, CV and references to The Branch Manager – PNG Motors Port Moresby
Email: [email protected]

Applications close 19 November 2025.

Only experienced sales professionals will be shortlisted.
