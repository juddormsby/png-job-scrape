Reach Stacker Operator

Share this job:


National Capital District, Papua New Guinea

Trade / Workshop

www.pngworkforce.com


Spectra Industrial Limited is seeking a dedicated and skilled Reach Stacker Operator to join our team at Spectra Industrial Ltd Port Moresby Branch. This position is crucial to our operations and plays a key role in ensuring the smooth functioning of our daily activities.

Position: Reach Stacker Operator

Location: Port Moresby Branch, Spectra Industrial Ltd

Reporting to: Workshop Operations Manager

Key Responsibilities:

  • Operating a reach stacker or a forklift (25ton plus) to transport materials and equipment
  • Loading and unloading trucks or storage zones with precision and care
  • Maintaining a clean and organized work environment
  • Adhering to all safety protocols and guidelines

Qualifications and Requirements:

  • Valid and current class Forklift driver's license
  • Previous experience operating a forklift (25ton plus) in an industrial setting
  • Experience operating a reach stacker
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to work efficiently
  • Good communication skills and ability to work well in a team
  • Physical fitness and ability to lift heavy objects

If you meet the above qualifications and are looking to join a dynamic team in a challenging yet rewarding environment, please submit your application including a resume and cover letter to email [email protected] or write to:

Spectra Industrial Ltd

P.O. Box 7528

Boroko NCD

Showroom location, Sect 57 Lot 01 Waigani Drive Tunnel

Application Deadline: 12th November 2025
