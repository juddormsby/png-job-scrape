Tebbutt Research PNG - Graduate Field Coordinator

  • Are you a young professional looking for an exciting and challenging career in the market and social research industry with a regional market leader and internationally recognised firm?
  • Do you have a degree qualification from a recognised university?
  • Do you have up to 2 years of commercial work experience?
  • Are you a motivated team player?

If this sounds like you, then you might be the person we are looking for!

The Company

Tebbutt Research is an ISO-20252-certified business that specialises in market and social research. We have been established for over 30 years and our clients include prominent multinational corporations, respected donor organizations and governments. We are the industry leader in the Pacific Islands, where we work across 22 countries. We have won numerous awards over the years and have a great, close-knit team and a supportive work culture.

Research fields include customer satisfaction, ad and communications testing, brand development, media ratings, segmentation studies, product testing, and monitoring and evaluation surveys. The techniques used include qualitative research, quantitative research, data analysis, observation, and audit methods.

The Role

We now have an opportunity for a professional to join our small executive team in Port Moresby as Graduate Field Coordinator. This role covers a range of project and people tasks both in the office and in the field, including:

  • coordination of our fieldwork teams of interviewers, including running project briefings, conducting field visits, monitoring team progress, and planning logistics
  • providing accurate and timely reporting on fieldwork status to the field management team
  • following our fieldwork compliance requirements for ISO using our existing procedures
  • regular travel domestically in PNG to conduct small team briefings and check on team progress
  • checking questionnaire scripts that are programmed onto tablet devices by our data team to see that they work correctly before running briefings with the fieldwork team
  • supporting the PNG General Manager as required

The role suits a person with flexibility in working hours, including work into early evenings and on weekends as necessary.

You will report to the PNG General Manager with line reporting to the Regional Operations Manager.

The Person

You must have a university degree in any field including business, with a good academic record.
You will ideally have up to 2 years of commercial work experience in any field, but newer graduates are also welcome to apply. An understanding of any of the following fields would be an advantage but not essential: market research, operations, information technology, project management, or human resources.

The role will suit you if you are interested in human behaviour, in learning to lead small teams of people, and logistics. You will have the following qualities:

  • a genuine interest in people’s views and opinions
  • the confidence to learn to lead teams
  • good communication skills and the ability to communicate with people from all walks of life
  • the highest level of personal integrity and honesty
  • a team player attitude and like working collaboratively
  • a quality focus
  • a high standard of written and spoken English

Details

Salary and benefits are commensurate with skills and experience.
