Project Officer - CIRCAR

Share this job:


Morobe, Papua New Guinea

Non Government Organisations (NGOs), Scientific

#24980

24th October 2025

www.pngworkforce.com


With more than 40 years of experience working in PNG, World Vision Papua New Guinea (WVPNG) focuses on helping the most vulnerable children and adults experience the fullness of life. Inspired by our Christian faith, WVPNG has an exciting opportunity for someone who may be looking for a change and wants to become part of a dynamic team.

We are currently looking for a suitable, qualified, experienced, and highly motivated professional to fill the Project Officer - CIRCAR to be based in Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea.

PURPOSE OF THE POSITION

The Project Officer – CIRCAR supports and coordinates implementation of nature-based solution activities under the Live and Learn–CIRCAR project. The role ensures that nature-based solution activities are effectively implemented with the grey solutions implemented by the ARUP, TSSP and Department of Works and Highways across project communities along the Markham highway in Lae, Morobe Province and particularly children and youth, are empowered to participate in consultation regarding which nature-based solution is practical. Under the supervision of the Area Program Manager, the Project Officer will provide effective and efficient support in planning, implementation, coordination, and management of the project.

MAJOR ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Project Implementation

  • Lead and support project implementation, and coordination, engagement with project participants and stakeholders, engage with Live and Learn in the planning of activities against the workplan and lead community engagements and capacity building initiatives.

Technical Support and capacity building

  • Provide training and mentoring to local partners, Village Health Assistants (VHAs), schools, and youth groups on climate adaptation, preparedness, and environmental stewardship and nature-based solutions.

Budget Management

  • Support the finance and admin team relating to project implementation, including preparation of cash requests and acquittals, ensure timely management and resource utilization in line with the developed Annual and Quarterly work plans and support the coordination and logistics for workshops and field visits

Monitoring, reporting & Learning

  • Collect and analyse data on NbS activities and indicators. Support participatory monitoring and documentation of lessons learned, case studies, and success stories for donor reporting

Community and Stakeholder Engagement

  • Strengthen coordination with partners and government. Facilitate community dialogues and youth participation in local NbS initiatives.

EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION/SKILLS, KNOWLEDGE & EXPERIENCE

  • Minimum 2–3 years’ experience in community development, climate change, or DRR programming.
  • Experience working with government agencies or international NGOs preferred.
  • Experience in engaging with partners and other stakeholders.
  • Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage personal workload and meet deadlines
  • Bachelor’s degree in Environmental Science, Climate Change, Development Studies, or related field.
  • Certification in Disaster Risk Reduction, Climate Adaptation, or Environmental Management is an asset
  • The position requires traveling to the Area program
  • Be available to work weekends and on short notice.
  • Physically fit with no underlying medical conditions.
  • Good communication skills in written and spoken English

Interested applicants should submit the following documents all in one PDF file before; 6 October 2025, 12:00 AM UTC

  • Expression of interest
  • Detailed CV
  • Valid Police & Medical Clearance Certificate

World Vision is committed to the principles of workplace diversity. Qualified women and people with disabilities are encouraged to apply.

World Vision is committed to the protection of children. We do not employ staff whose background is not aligned with our child protection practices. Hence employment is conditional upon successful completion of all applicable background checks, including criminal record checks, where possible.

We appreciate the time it takes to prepare your application. Please note that only short-listed candidates suitable for interviews will be contacted. If you do not hear from World Vision within two (2) weeks of the closing date, please consider your application unsuccessful.
