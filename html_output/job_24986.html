National Capital District, Papua New Guinea

Airline / Airport / Travel, Carpenter, Carpentry, Operations Support

#24986

27th October 2025

www.pngworkforce.com


PNG Air, renowned for its extensive network covering over 26 destinations throughout Papua New Guinea, operates the largest fleet of ATR 72-600 series aircraft in the region. As the Airline of Choice, we're committed to delivering a world-class experience with competitive fares, unparalleled cargo rates, and a focus on safety and on-time performance for our valued customers and stakeholders.

CARPENTER

This position will report to the Chief Executive Officer and is responsible for the maintenance of the company’s buildings, furniture and overall repair and upkeep of all company assets.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Conduct regular maintenance of the company’s building and structures, i.e. carpentry and related responsibilities on a day to day basis
  • Conduct repair work and installation of doors, windows, drywall, cabinets, stairs, and other fixtures as required
  • Responsible for upgrades associated with PNG Air properties and those leased from NAC
  • Provide excellent customer service

QUALIFICATIONS/EXPERIENCES

  • General Airline experience with thorough knowledge in airline commercial/private operations
  • Additional certification may be required
  • Good communication skills.
  • Physically fit with good hand-eye coordination.
  • Tertiary qualifications in related discipline(s) desirable but not essential
